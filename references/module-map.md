# Marketing Module Map

Use this map to route work. Read the selected installed skill's `SKILL.md` before using it. Preserve all modules; select only the ones needed in a given task.

## Foundation

- `product-marketing`: Establish product, audience, jobs, alternatives, differentiation, objections, proof, and goals. Use first on a new project.

## Research and Strategy

- `customer-research`: Interviews, surveys, review mining, VOC, JTBD, personas, and research synthesis.
- `competitor-profiling`: Source-backed competitor dossiers, pricing, positioning, reviews, and landscape summaries.
- `marketing-plan`: Integrate goals, audience, positioning, channels, programs, budget, metrics, and roadmap.
- `marketing-council`: Generate multiple expert perspectives when a strategic decision benefits from constructive disagreement.
- `marketing-ideas`: Expand the option set before prioritization.
- `marketing-psychology`: Apply behavioral principles without replacing customer evidence.
- `offers`: Design the value exchange, offer structure, risk reversal, urgency, and bonuses.
- `pricing`: Analyze willingness to pay, packaging, price metrics, tiers, and monetization trade-offs.
- `launch`: Plan phases, channels, assets, sequencing, and launch measurement.

## Conversion and Lifecycle

- `cro`: Diagnose and improve marketing pages and forms.
- `signup`: Optimize account creation and trial-entry flows.
- `onboarding`: Improve activation, first-run experience, and time to value.
- `paywalls`: Optimize upgrade prompts and in-product monetization moments.
- `popups`: Design contextual overlays and capture experiences.
- `ab-testing`: Form hypotheses, variants, metrics, sample logic, and experiment plans.
- `analytics`: Define events, properties, funnels, attribution, and implementation checks.
- `churn-prevention`: Diagnose churn and design save, recovery, and retention motions.

## Content and Creative

- `copywriting`: Produce research-informed page and campaign copy.
- `copy-editing`: Improve clarity, specificity, voice, and conversion of existing copy.
- `emails`: Create lifecycle and automated email sequences.
- `cold-email`: Write concise personalized outbound sequences.
- `sms`: Plan compliant SMS and MMS messaging.
- `social`: Create channel-appropriate social strategy and content.
- `video`: Plan and produce video concepts and scripts.
- `image`: Plan or generate marketing imagery.
- `ad-creative`: Generate and iterate performance creative variants.
- `content-strategy`: Choose topics, formats, distribution, and editorial priorities.
- `lead-magnets`: Design useful assets that earn contact information.
- `free-tools`: Evaluate and build utility-led acquisition tools.
- `community-marketing`: Build or participate in communities for durable growth.

## SEO and Discovery

- `seo-audit`: Diagnose technical and on-page organic search issues.
- `ai-seo`: Improve visibility and citation in AI-generated answers.
- `programmatic-seo`: Design scalable, data-backed page systems.
- `site-architecture`: Structure navigation, page hierarchy, URLs, and internal links.
- `schema`: Implement and validate structured data.
- `aso`: Optimize App Store and Google Play discovery and conversion.
- `competitors`: Create comparison and alternative pages from verified profiles.
- `directory-submissions`: Select and submit to relevant product directories.

## Distribution and Growth

- `ads`: Plan and optimize paid acquisition campaigns.
- `co-marketing`: Identify partners and design joint campaigns.
- `referrals`: Design referral, affiliate, and word-of-mouth programs.
- `public-relations`: Develop stories, media targets, pitches, and earned-media plans.
- `prospecting`: Build, qualify, and prioritize outreach lists.

## Sales and Operations

- `revops`: Design lead stages, scoring, routing, handoffs, pipeline, and reporting.
- `sales-enablement`: Turn strategy into decks, battle cards, one-pagers, objection handling, and demo narratives.

## Agentic Operations

- `marketing-loops`: Design repeatable, measurable marketing workflows that an agent can run over time.

## Common Multi-Module Routes

- GTM launch: `product-marketing` -> `customer-research` + `competitor-profiling` -> `pricing` -> `launch` -> `analytics`.
- Landing-page validation: `customer-research` -> `copywriting` -> `cro` -> `analytics` -> `ab-testing`.
- Creator or partner motion: `customer-research` -> `prospecting` + `co-marketing` -> `cold-email` -> `analytics`.
- Full marketing plan: `product-marketing` -> `marketing-plan`, then route to channel and lifecycle modules.
- Competitive sales motion: `competitor-profiling` -> `sales-enablement` -> `cold-email` or `ads`.
